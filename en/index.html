Features

Everything you need to keep your rhythm

Open the app and you see what's next, how today is going and the goals you're working toward.

  • Your schedule in a minute

    13 ready-made templates for students, office workers, factory workers, night-shift nurses and more. Enter your wake-up time and bedtime and the template shifts to fit you.

  • Reminders & alarms on time

    Heads-ups 5, 10 or 15 minutes ahead, even when the app is closed. Wake-up alarms ring at the exact minute and ring again if you haven't opened the app.

  • Rotating shift schedules

    2 days – 2 nights – 2 off, weekly rotating shifts, 4 on 2 off… Each routine shows up only on its shift days, and streak-safe breaks cover holidays and sick days.

  • Lunar calendar repeats

    The 1st and 15th of the lunar month, any lunar day each month, or a yearly date on the lunar calendar.

  • Focus & goals

    A Focus Timer for every activity and countdowns to an exam or a race. Running late? “Re-plan the rest of the day” fits what's left into the free time before bed.

  • Sleep better

    Wind-down mode with a bedtime checklist, 4-7-8 breathing and a countdown to bedtime. Read your sleep from a smartwatch through Health Connect.

  • Streaks & real rewards

    Milestone badges, a Streak Freeze when you miss a day, and schedule suggestions based on three weeks of your real habits, worked out on your phone.

  • Photo check-ins & widgets

    Snap a photo when you finish an activity to get a check-in card and a monthly film. Home screen widgets show your progress, streak and what's next.

Your routine stays on your phone
